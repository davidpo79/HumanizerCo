FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Does HumanizerCo really bypass AI detectors? +

Yes, our rewrites consistently score below the AI-probability threshold on GPTZero, Turnitin, Originality.ai, and similar tools. We focus on rhythm, vocabulary diversity, and sentence structure variance, which are the signals detectors look for.

Is my text stored or used for training? +

No. Your text is processed in memory and discarded immediately. We don't train on user input, ever. We don't keep logs of content.

What languages are supported? +

English is fully supported today. Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese are in beta on the Pro plan. More languages are added monthly.

Is the free plan really free? +

Yes. 3 humanizations per day, no credit card required, no time limit. Upgrade only if you need more volume or longer passes.

Will the rewrite still sound like me? +

That's exactly the goal. The Pro plan includes style memory. Feed us a few samples of your real writing and we'll match cadence, vocabulary, and quirks. Free plan uses sensible defaults that read as a thoughtful, slightly informal human.
